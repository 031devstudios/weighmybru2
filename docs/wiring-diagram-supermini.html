Build notes

The switch cuts the negative lead

Battery − runs through the slide switch to the board's BAT− pad. Battery + goes straight to BAT+. Use the JST-PH 2.0 connector on the battery leads so you can unplug the battery.

Battery sense divider

Two 100 kΩ resistors split BAT+ in half for GPIO7. The firmware multiplies the reading by 2.0 (VOLTAGE_DIVIDER_RATIO), so a full 4.2 V cell reads 2.1 V at the pin. Use 1% resistors, or the battery percentage drifts.

Touch pads are TTP223 modules

The firmware reads GPIO3 and GPIO4 with digitalRead and an internal pull-down. Each pad needs a TTP223 module with SIG, VCC and GND. A bare copper pad will not work.

Both touch pins do extra jobs

GPIO3 wakes the scale from deep sleep. Holding the tare pad (GPIO4) during boot clears the saved WiFi credentials.

Everything runs on 3.3 V

HX711, OLED and both touch modules take power from the 3V3 pin. Leave the 5V pin unconnected.

Negative weight readings?

Swap the white and green load cell wires on A− and A+.

XIAO ESP32S3

The firmware uses the same GPIO numbers on the XIAO, but the pins sit in different physical spots. See wiring-diagram-xiao.pdf for that board.
