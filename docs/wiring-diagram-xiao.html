Build notes

D4 and D5 go to the HX711, not the OLED

Seeed's pinout labels D4 and D5 as SDA and SCL. This firmware ignores those labels: D4 and D5 drive the HX711, and the OLED uses D9 (SDA) and D10 (SCL). Wiring the OLED to D4/D5 leaves the screen blank.

Battery pads are on the back

Solder the battery leads to the BAT+ and BAT− pads on the underside of the XIAO. The XIAO has a built-in charger, so the battery charges over USB-C. The slide switch sits on the negative lead, the same as the SuperMini build.

Battery sense divider

The XIAO does not route its battery voltage to an ADC pin, so you still need the two 100 kΩ resistors. Their midpoint goes to D8 (GPIO7). The firmware multiplies the reading by 2.0, so a full 4.2 V cell reads 2.1 V at the pin.

Touch pads are TTP223 modules

The firmware reads D2 and D3 with digitalRead and an internal pull-down. Each pad needs a TTP223 module with SIG, VCC and GND.

Both touch pins do extra jobs

D2 wakes the scale from deep sleep. Holding the tare pad (D3) during boot clears the saved WiFi credentials.

Unused pins

Leave 5V, D0, D1, D6 and D7 unconnected.

Flash the XIAO firmware

Use the esp32s3-xiao build. It sets BOARD_XIAO and the 8 MB flash layout.
